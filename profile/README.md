# Roman Galaxy Redshift Survey — Project Infrastructure Team (GRS PIT)

The **Galaxy Redshift Survey Project Infrastructure Team (GRS PIT)** develops the simulations, software, and science-ready data products needed to realize the cosmology goals of NASA's **Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope** Galaxy Redshift Survey. Led by **Yun Wang (Caltech/IPAC)**, the team supports the Roman High Latitude Wide Area Survey (HLWAS) and the broader scientific community.

## Science overview

Roman's near-infrared slitless spectroscopy will map galaxy redshifts and large-scale structure across a wide area of sky. The GRS PIT is building tools to model survey selection and systematics and measure galaxy clustering—including **baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO)** and **redshift-space distortions (RSD)**—to constrain cosmic expansion, the growth of structure, and dark energy.

## Planned data products and software

| Product | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Value-added galaxy catalogs | Redshift-quality metrics, classifications, and derived galaxy properties |
| Large-scale-structure catalogs | Galaxy and random catalogs for clustering analyses |
| Clustering statistics and covariances | Two- and three-point measurements, covariance and window products |
| Galaxy mock catalogs | Simulated galaxy populations for survey analysis and forecasting |
| Grism simulations | Realistic simulated Roman slitless-spectroscopy observations |
| Public analysis software | Tools for masking, survey footprints, clustering, covariance modeling, and simulations |

**Planned releases:** prelaunch tools and simulations, followed by products accompanying Roman data releases (DR1–DR3). Specific deliverables and timing may evolve.
